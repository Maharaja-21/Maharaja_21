Student Biometric Attendance System

A college-oriented attendance application designed to support student identity verification and attendance management.

Problem: manual roll calls take class time and are easy to get wrong or misuse. Overview: students sign in, complete a face-verification step, and attendance is recorded with a clear result.

  • Student authentication
  • Face-verification workflow
  • Attendance management
  • Clear verification result

Security note: face matching alone does not guarantee liveness or stop photo-based spoofing. This project is not production-secure until liveness checks and other controls are implemented and tested.
